Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

TETRAform ("we", "our", "us") is a Chrome extension that fills web forms from profiles you save. This policy explains what data the extension handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your profiles, fields and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.
  • There is no account, no tracking and no analytics inside the extension.
  • Data leaves your device only when you send the optional feedback form. Details are in section 3.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Data stored on your device

The extension saves the following with Chrome's storage API (chrome.storage.local). It stays in your browser and is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Profiles: the details you save to fill forms, such as name, email, phone, address, work history and any custom fields you create.
  • Settings: your active profile, domain-specific profiles, sites where you turned TETRAform off, and display preferences.
  • Fields to review: when you save fields from a page, TETRAform remembers which fields were new, plus that page's title and address (without any query string), so you can review them in Settings. Cleared when you press "Done reviewing".
  • Daily fill counter: how many fills you used today, to apply the Free plan limit. It is a number only and never leaves your device.

If you export a JSON backup, the file is saved where you choose. We never receive it.

2. What we never collect

  • Passwords, card numbers, CVV codes, CAPTCHA answers and one-time codes. TETRAform blocks these fields and never saves them.
  • Your browsing history or the pages you visit.
  • The contents of forms you fill. Pages are read on your device only, to match fields to your profile.
  • Usage analytics, advertising IDs or tracking cookies inside the extension.

3. The optional feedback form

The Contact & Feedback form in the extension settings is the only feature that sends data off your device. Nothing is sent unless you write a message and press Send. When you do, we receive:

DataWhy
Feedback type and your messageTo read and act on your feedback
Your email address (optional)Only to reply to you. Leave it empty to stay anonymous.
Extension version and plan (Free or Pro)To reproduce and fix bugs
Browser user agent, platform and languageTo reproduce and fix bugs
Time of submissionTo track and answer requests in order

Your saved profiles are never included. Please don't put personal details in the message that you don't want us to see.

The feedback is handled by these service providers, over encrypted HTTPS connections:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) stores the submission in a private database that only we can read.
  • Cloudflare (Workers and Email) forwards a copy of the submission to our inbox by email. Cloudflare briefly processes your IP address to block spam and abuse; we do not store it.
  • Our email provider holds the forwarded copy in our mailbox.

We keep feedback only as long as we need it to respond and improve TETRAform.

How to delete your feedback

  1. Email contact@tetraform.app with the subject "Delete my feedback".
  2. Send it from the email address you entered in the feedback form. That address is how we find your submissions.
  3. We delete them from our database and our inbox, and confirm by reply.

The email field in the form is optional. If you sent feedback without an email, we have no way to tell which submission is yours, so we can't find it to delete it.

4. Permissions

  • storage: to save your profiles and settings on your device.
  • activeTab and scripting: to fill the form on the page you are on when you ask it to.
  • Access to all sites (<all_urls>): forms appear on any website, so TETRAform checks pages for form fields to show the Assistant Bar. This check runs on your device. We do not record which sites you visit.

5. Sharing and Limited Use

We do not sell, rent or transfer your data to third parties, except the service providers in section 3 that process feedback on our behalf. We do not use data for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose unrelated to TETRAform.

TETRAform's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your choices

  • Delete local data: delete profiles in the extension settings, or remove the extension to erase everything it stored.
  • Stay anonymous: send feedback without an email address, or don't use the form at all.
  • Access or delete feedback: follow the steps in section 3. We will tell you what we hold or delete it.

7. This website

This policy covers the extension. The tetraform.app website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful, only if you accept analytics cookies in the cookie banner. Until then, the Google Analytics script does not load and nothing is sent to Google. When allowed, Google Analytics sets cookies and receives standard browser information such as your IP address. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer; rejecting removes the analytics cookies. Google Analytics is not used inside the extension and is never linked to your profiles.

8. Security

  • Local data is kept in Chrome's extension storage, separate from websites.
  • Feedback is sent over HTTPS. The feedback database blocks all reads from the extension, so submissions can't be read back by anyone else.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how TETRAform handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change ships. Features that would send profile data off your device, such as the planned Pro cloud sync, will be opt-in and described here first.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@tetraform.app or use the feedback form in the extension settings.
